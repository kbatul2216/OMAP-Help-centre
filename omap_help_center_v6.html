Common questions about getting started
Do I need an MRM to get started?▼
No. The Concierge Agent and L&D Agent work without an MRM. You only need an MRM for the Collections Agent and Member Audit Agent. Start with Concierge or L&D while your MRM integration is being set up.

Creating Your OMAP Account

A step-by-step guide to registering your account and completing email verification before you can access the platform.

Time: Under 3 minutes
Steps: 2
Overview
What this page covers

Creating an OMAP account involves two stages. First, you fill in your registration form. Second, you verify your email using a one-time password (OTP). Your account is not active until both stages are complete.

Make sure the email address you register with is immediately accessible — the OTP is sent there right away and expires quickly.

Step-by-step guide
Complete both steps in order. You cannot log in until Step 2 is done.
1
Fill in the registration form

Navigate to the OMAP sign-up page. Work through every field in the registration form. All fields are required — the Sign Up button stays disabled until every field is filled and the Terms & Conditions checkbox is ticked.

Pay special attention to your email address — this is where your OTP will be sent immediately after you click Sign Up. Your password must be entered twice and both entries must match exactly.

View all required fields ▾
Personal information
  • Full Name — Used to personalize your workspace
  • Email Address — Used for login, OTP, and all notifications. Must be valid and accessible.
  • Phone Number — Your contact number including country code

Business information
  • Company Name — The organization you represent
  • Job Title — Your role within the company
  • Industry — Select from the dropdown
  • Website URL — Your company website. OMAP may use this to suggest agent branding.

Security
  • Password — Create a strong password (minimum 8 characters recommended)
  • Confirm Password — Must match the Password field exactly. Case-sensitive.

The Terms & Conditions checkbox must be ticked before the Sign Up button activates.
2
Verify your email with OTP

Immediately after clicking Sign Up, OMAP sends a 6-digit One-Time Password (OTP) to your registered email. You are simultaneously redirected to the verification screen — do not close this page.

Open your inbox, find the email from OMAP, copy the 6-digit code, enter it on the verification screen, and click Verify. Your account activates instantly and you are redirected to the platform.

!
OTP expires quickly. If it expires before you enter it, click Resend OTP to get a new code. Always check your spam or junk folder if the email doesn't arrive within 2 minutes. Only the most recently sent code is valid.
Frequently Asked Questions
Common issues during sign-up and email verification
Why is the Sign Up button still disabled after I filled in everything?▼
The most common reason is that the Terms & Conditions checkbox has not been ticked. Scroll to the bottom of the form and check the box. Also confirm that your Password and Confirm Password fields match exactly — even one character difference keeps the button disabled.
I didn't receive the OTP email — what should I do?▼
Check your spam, junk, or promotions folder first. Make sure you are checking the exact email address you registered with. If still nothing after 2–3 minutes, click Resend OTP on the verification screen to request a fresh code.
My OTP expired before I could enter it — what now?▼
OTPs are time-limited for security. Click Resend OTP on the verification screen to generate a new code. Do not use an old code — only the most recently issued one is valid.
Can I use the same email address for multiple OMAP accounts?▼
No. Each email address can only be linked to one OMAP account. If you try to sign up with an already-registered email, you will see an error. Go to the Login page instead if you already have an account.
What happens right after I verify my email?▼
Your account is activated instantly and you are automatically redirected to the OMAP platform workspace. The next step is to log in using your registered email and the password you set during sign-up.
Can I update my business information after signing up?▼
Yes. Go to the top-right user menu and click Edit Profile. From there you can update your name, phone number, company details, and password. Your registered email address is not editable after account creation.
Ready to log in?
Your account is verified. Follow the Login guide to access your workspace.
Go to Login →

Logging in to OMAP

A complete guide to accessing your OMAP workspace — including the standard login flow and how to reset your password if you've forgotten it.

Time: Under 1 minute
Requires: A verified account
Overview
Before you begin

You need a verified OMAP account to log in. If you have not completed sign-up and OTP verification yet, complete the Sign Up guide first — login will fail for unverified accounts.

The login page is straightforward: enter your registered email and password, then click Login. If your credentials are correct, you are immediately redirected to your workspace.

Standard login — step by step
Three steps to access your OMAP workspace.
1
Enter your email address

Enter the exact email address you used when creating your OMAP account. The field is not case-sensitive, but the address must match your registered email exactly.

2
Enter your password

Enter your account password. Passwords in OMAP are case-sensitive. Check that Caps Lock is off if you are getting login errors. If you have forgotten your password, skip to the Forgot Password section below.

3
Click Login

Click the Login button. If your credentials are correct, you are redirected to the Choose Your AI Agent page — the main workspace. If incorrect, an "Invalid credentials" error appears. Double-check your email and password and try again.

Login outcomes
Correct credentials You are logged in and redirected to the Choose Your AI Agent workspace.
Incorrect credentials An "Invalid credentials" error appears. Check email and password and try again.
Forgot your password? — Reset flow
Reset your password securely in two steps.
A
Request a reset link

On the login page, click the Forgot Password? link below the Login button. Enter the email address associated with your OMAP account and click Send Reset Link. OMAP will email you a secure, time-limited reset link.

B
Set a new password

Open the email from OMAP and click the reset link. Enter your new password, confirm it, and click Update Password. You will receive a security confirmation email after every password change. You are then redirected to the login page.

i
Security tip: If you receive a password-change confirmation email that you did not initiate, contact OMAP support immediately — your account may have been accessed without authorization.
Frequently Asked Questions
Common login issues and how to resolve them
I keep getting "Invalid credentials" — what's wrong?▼
There are three common causes: (1) You are using the wrong email — use the exact email you signed up with. (2) Your password has a typo — passwords are case-sensitive, check for Caps Lock. (3) Your account is not yet verified — complete OTP verification from sign-up before attempting to log in.
I forgot my password — how do I reset it?▼
Click Forgot Password? on the login page. Enter your registered email and click Send Reset Link. Check your inbox (and spam folder) for the reset email, click the link, set a new password, confirm it, and click Update Password. You are then redirected to log in with your new password.
The password reset link in my email doesn't work.▼
Password reset links are time-limited and expire after a short period. If the link is expired, return to the Forgot Password page and request a new link. Always use the link from the most recently sent email — older links are invalid once a new one has been issued.
After logging in, where do I land?▼
After a successful login you are redirected to the Choose Your AI Agent page — the main workspace where all available agent types are displayed. From here you can create a new agent or access an existing one.
I received a password-change email but didn't change my password.▼
This is a security alert. Use the Forgot Password flow immediately to reset your password to something new, and contact OMAP support to report the incident. Your account may have been accessed without your authorization.

Concierge Agent — Overview

Your AI-powered virtual front desk. Answers member questions 24/7 about memberships, schedules, billing, and policies. Supports text and optional voice in English, Spanish, and French.

📋 4 setup steps
⏱ ~15 min
🔗 MRM optional
What is the Concierge Agent?
Purpose and core capabilities

The Concierge Agent acts like a digital receptionist for your business — available 24/7 through a branded chat widget embedded on your website. Instead of staff answering the same questions repeatedly, the agent handles member conversations automatically.

You control how it looks (branding, colors, logo), how it communicates (tone, language, voice), and what it knows (your website content and uploaded documents). When connected to an MRM, it can also authenticate members and answer account-specific questions.

❓ FAQs — Concierge Agent
General questions about the Concierge Agent
Does the Concierge Agent work without an MRM?▼
Yes — it answers general questions from its knowledge base (website content and documents) without any MRM. MRM is only needed for account-specific features like billing lookups and membership queries.
What languages does it support?▼
English (default), Spanish, and French. You can enable multiple languages in Step 2 (Agent Behavior). Voice mode also supports all three languages.
Can I update the agent after it's deployed?▼
Yes — use Edit Agent from the sidebar. Changes create a new draft. Use Version History to compare and publish when ready. The live agent updates immediately after publishing.

Agent Behavior

Define how your Concierge Agent communicates — its greeting, tone of voice, behavior style, supported languages, voice mode, and custom response guidelines.

Overview
What this step controls

While Step 1 controls how the agent looks, Step 2 controls how it sounds and behaves. This is where you define the agent's personality. The choices you make here shape every response the agent gives to your members.

The Response Guidelines field is especially powerful — it lets you write custom rules that the agent follows in every conversation, giving you precise control over accuracy, tone, and compliance.

Configuration fields
All fields in Step 2 explained.
1
First Greeting Message

The very first message members see when they open the chat. It should feel natural and on-brand. Example: "Hello! I'm Wellness Wendy from GymCo. How can I assist you today?"

2
Tone of Voice & Behavior Style

Tone: Professional · Friendly · Casual · Formal · Enthusiastic
Behavior: Helpful · Direct · Empathetic · Analytical · Conversational & Warm

Match these to your brand's communication standards.

3
Language & Voice Mode

English is selected by default. Optionally add Spanish and/or French. Enable Voice Mode to unlock agent voice selection and per-language voice preview.

4
Response Guidelines

Write custom rules that control every agent response. Examples:

"Always verify club location before sharing pricing"
"Only reference links from the official website"
"Be empathetic when a member asks about cancellation"

ℹ️ More guideline examples ▾
  • "Keep answers under 3 sentences unless asked for more detail"
  • "Always greet returning members by name if known"
  • "Do not share competitor pricing or information"
  • "If unsure, direct the member to call the front desk"
❓ FAQs — Step 2
Common questions about agent behavior settings
What's the difference between Tone and Behavior Style?▼
Tone affects how the agent sounds (e.g. formal vs casual). Behavior Style affects how it approaches helping users (e.g. direct vs empathetic). Both work together to shape the agent's overall personality.
Can I add response guidelines after deployment?▼
Yes — use Edit Agent → Step 2 to update guidelines at any time. Save, then publish the new version from Version History.

Agent Deployment

The final creation step — generate your widget code snippet and paste it into your website to make the Concierge Agent live for all visitors.

What happens in this step
From configuration to live on your website.

After completing Steps 1–3, the platform generates a unique widget code snippet for your agent. You copy this code and paste it into your website's HTML, just before the closing </body> tag. Once added, the chat widget appears live for all your website visitors.

Before pasting the code, always complete a test run and website preview to confirm everything looks and works as expected.

Deployment steps
Follow in order for a smooth go-live.
1
Test the agent first

Use the Test Agent button to interact with your agent inside the platform. Verify the greeting, tone, and a few member questions before deploying live.

2
Preview on your website

Use Website Preview to see exactly how the chat widget looks on your site — checking placement, branding, and mobile responsiveness.

3
Copy and paste the widget code

Copy the generated script tag and paste it into your website HTML before the closing </body> tag. The agent is immediately live after saving your website.

❓ FAQs — Deployment
Common questions about going live
Where exactly do I paste the widget code?▼
Paste the script tag just before the closing </body> tag in your website's main HTML file. For CMS platforms (WordPress, Squarespace, etc.), use the site-wide footer scripts section.
The widget isn't appearing on my website — why?▼
Check that the script tag is placed correctly in the HTML, the domain matches, and the agent has been published (not just saved as a draft). Clear your browser cache and try again.

Test Agent

Interact with your Concierge Agent inside the OMAP platform before it goes live. Verify greeting, tone, answers, and voice (if enabled).

How to test your agent
Use the internal sandbox to evaluate agent quality.

The Test Agent interface opens a Sandbox chat session inside OMAP. These conversations don't count towards your plan's conversation limit and won't appear in Live Conversation Logs — they're labeled "Sandbox" so you can easily distinguish them later.

1
Open Test Agent

From the Concierge Agent left sidebar, click Test Agent. The sandbox chat interface opens immediately.

2
Run test conversations

Ask the agent questions your real members would ask. Check: greeting message accuracy, tone consistency, knowledge base answer quality, and voice mode (if enabled).

3
Identify and fix gaps

If the agent gives wrong or incomplete answers, return to Edit Agent → Step 3 to update the knowledge base. Re-test after updating. Repeat until satisfied.

❓ FAQs — Test Agent
Questions about the testing process
Do sandbox conversations count towards my plan limit?▼
No — Sandbox conversations are internal test sessions and do not consume your plan's conversation quota. Only Live conversations (from real website visitors) count.
The agent gives wrong answers in testing — what should I do?▼
Add more relevant URLs or documents in Edit Agent → Knowledge Base. Use Refresh Agent to reprocess the content. Then test again until answers are accurate.

✏️ Edit Agent

Edit Agent — Overview

Update your Concierge Agent's configuration at any time — UI, behavior, or knowledge base. Changes create a new draft version that you review and publish safely.

How Edit Agent works
Safe, version-controlled updates.

Every change you make via Edit Agent creates a new draft version — the live agent is never altered until you explicitly publish. This protects you from accidentally breaking a running agent.

1
Click Edit Agent from the sidebar

This opens all three creation steps (UI, Behavior, Knowledge Base) in edit mode with your current configuration pre-filled.

2
Make your changes

Update any field across Steps 1, 2, or 3. Changes are saved as a draft automatically.

3
Compare and publish via Version History

Go to Settings → Version History. Compare the draft vs the live version — changes are marked as Added, Edited, or Deleted. Click Publish Draft to make it live.

❓ FAQs — Edit Agent
Common questions about editing
Does editing the agent take it offline?▼
No — editing creates a draft version. The live agent continues running uninterrupted until you choose to publish the draft.
Can I discard a draft without publishing it?▼
Yes — in Version History, you can discard the draft to revert to the last published version without any changes going live.

General platform
What is OMAP™ by ALTA·DX?▼
OMAP (Outcomes Micro Agent Platform) is an AI-powered platform for creating and deploying specialized AI agents — each focused on a specific business outcome. It uses micro-agent architecture where each agent handles a single function.
Do I need an MRM to use OMAP?▼
No — Concierge and L&D agents work without an MRM. Collections and Member Audit agents require a connected MRM. Start with Concierge or L&D while your MRM integration is being configured.
My agent stopped responding — what do I do?▼
Check Settings → Billing. If your conversation limit is reached, the agent stops immediately. Upgrade your plan to restore it. Also confirm the agent is published (not just a draft) and the widget script is correctly embedded.
How do I update an agent after deployment?▼
Use Edit Agent from the sidebar. Changes create a new draft. Use Version History to compare and click Publish Draft when ready. The live agent updates immediately.
What's the difference between Sandbox and Live conversations?▼
Sandbox = internal test sessions (don't count towards plan limits). Live = real member conversations from the deployed website widget. Both appear in Conversation Logs with their labels.
